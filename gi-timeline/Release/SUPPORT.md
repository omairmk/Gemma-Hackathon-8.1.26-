## Product support

Use GI Journal product support for installation, app access, local journal behavior, manual photo attachment, entry editing, dated no-bowel-movement records, PDF creation, or accessibility problems.

Do not send journal photos, exported PDFs, symptoms, notes, model prompts or output, or other sensitive health information. GI Journal has no developer account, remote journal store, analytics feed, or server-side journal-recreation capability, so support cannot automatically inspect, access, or recreate the in-app journal. If a person voluntarily contacts support, the support provider processes only the correspondence that person chooses to send under the published support/privacy terms.

## Medical and emergency questions

Product support cannot interpret an entry, diagnose a condition, assess severity, recommend treatment, or respond to an emergency. Contact a qualified clinician for medical questions. Use local emergency services for an emergency.

## Important local-data limitation

GI Journal stores app-owned journal records, retained photo copies, drafts, and provenance in its protected local container and does not upload them to the developer. GI Journal requests exclusion of live journal files from automatic device backup; iOS controls backup behavior. There is no iCloud journal database, developer-managed cloud sync, developer-accessible backup, or V1 journal-transfer feature. Same-install unfinished-draft reopening and fail-closed journal retry remain local safeguards. GI Journal does not intentionally delete saved logs during an ordinary app close/reopen, iPhone restart followed by unlock, or in-place app update; iOS and storage integrity still govern retention. Deleting or reinstalling GI Journal, erasing, losing, resetting, or replacing the iPhone, or a storage failure may permanently lose entries and photos. Support cannot restore them. A PDF is readable but cannot restore app data or create journal entries.

## Offline behavior

The intended public fallback is designed so app-owned journal, camera photo attachment, manual entry, editing, no-bowel-movement logging, and PDF functions do not require the developer's server after installation. It does not analyze or prefill photos and the public app contains no model payload. Selecting an iCloud-only item in Apple Photos can require connectivity before Photos supplies that item. Opening this support page or other external legal links requires connectivity.
