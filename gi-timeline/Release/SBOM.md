## Known inventory gap

`CLiteRTLM` is a prebuilt native XCFramework. A direct package license does not enumerate every statically linked or bundled transitive component. The final SBOM must therefore add results from:

- Swift package dependency resolution;
- archive/framework/resource enumeration;
- binary linkage and symbol/package inspection where appropriate;
- embedded `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, acknowledgements, and privacy manifests;
- upstream release provenance for the exact XCFramework; and
- legal review of transitive obligations.

Likely technologies mentioned by upstream documentation are not listed as shipped components until verified in the exact binary.

Historical v0.14 symbol-family evidence is recorded in `RUNTIME_NOTICES_GAP_INVENTORY_BUILD8_2026-08-03.md`. Exact v0.15 package/framework evidence and the strict boundary between verified direct notices and unbound static-component indicators are recorded in `RUNTIME_NOTICES_GAP_INVENTORY_BUILD8_V015_2026-08-04.md`. Neither ledger assigns guessed licenses or closes this gap.

## Final-generation procedure

1. From clean frozen source, validate the local derivative wrapper and the intentionally empty-pin `Package.resolved`, resolve the checksum-pinned v0.15.0 binary target into a unique cache, and retain the resolved-artifact receipt.
2. Build/archive the AppStore configuration from the final commit.
3. Export and enumerate every application, framework, library, bundle, manifest, executable, and legal resource; explicitly prove that no model payload or `EmbeddedModels` directory exists.
4. Record SHA-256 hashes for the app archive, embedded frameworks, and legal resource bundle, plus the model-payload-absence validation receipt.
5. Generate a machine-readable CycloneDX or SPDX SBOM with the approved release tool.
6. Reconcile that output with this direct-component ledger and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
7. Sign the SBOM/release record and retain it with the submitted build.
